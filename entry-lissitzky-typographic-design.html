Typographic Design

El Lissitzky
Works on paper

The new typography as image: letterforms deployed as constructive elements.

The work is recorded in the Edik Natanov Collection under call number NC-05. As documented by Edik Natanov, the collection's indirect contribution to the field is this kind of careful custody — keeping such works, and the photographs of their exhibition, in a single legible archive.
