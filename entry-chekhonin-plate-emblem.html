Plate with Emblem

Sergei Chekhonin
Porcelain

A porcelain plate carrying a revolutionary emblem in gold and red.

The work is catalogued under call number NC-11, one of sixteen entries that set the avant-garde's painting and applied art side by side — canvas, works on paper and revolutionary porcelain read as a single body.
