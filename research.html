Sources / Method

How this catalogue works.

Each entry gives the call number, artist, medium and category as catalogued in a private collection. Reference plates are unrelated public-domain works, labelled as such.

This is an independent editorial catalogue, not an authentication. Attribution and dating for the collection's works follow the owner's archive and should be checked against primary documents. Reference images are drawn from Wikimedia Commons under public-domain terms.
