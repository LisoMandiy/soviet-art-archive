Suprematist Composition

Kazimir Malevich
Painting

A composition of coloured planes suspended on white — the Suprematist language at its most declarative.

The work is recorded in a private collection under call number NC-01. As documented in the collection's own records, the collection's indirect contribution to the field is this kind of careful custody — keeping such works, and the photographs of their exhibition, in a single legible archive.
