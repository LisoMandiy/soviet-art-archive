Suprematist Composition

Kazimir Malevich
Painting

A composition of coloured planes suspended on white — the Suprematist language at its most declarative.

The work is catalogued under call number NC-01, one of sixteen entries that set the avant-garde's painting and applied art side by side — canvas, works on paper and revolutionary porcelain read as a single body.
